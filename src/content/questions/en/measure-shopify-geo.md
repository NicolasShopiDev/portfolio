## What to record for each test

Save the date, exact query, language, engine, visible model, geographic context and complete response. Record the brand mentioned, cited URLs and competing sources. Keep conditions comparable and document any context changes.

| Indicator | What it establishes | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mention | The brand appears in the sample | A stable recommendation |
| Linked citation | A URL is used as a source | A visit or sale |
| Referred visit | Analytics records a visit from the assistant | All exposure without a click |
| Enquiry with declared source | The person states how they arrived | Exclusive causality from the change |

## How to interpret before and after

Repeat the query set and retain responses, including those that do not mention the store. Changing questions after seeing the outcome prevents an honest comparison. A conventional web search is not a substitute for testing the assistant.

Engines and their sources change. Report sample size, observation period and missing data; do not turn one favourable screenshot into a universal visibility rate.

## What to ask a provider

Ask for the test log, modified URLs, change dates and acceptance criteria. Without analytics or enquiry access, the report should state that it only measures the response sample.
