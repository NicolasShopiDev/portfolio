## When to prioritise technical SEO

If a collection is not indexable, links point to the wrong URLs or product content is absent from HTML, start with those issues. An additional editorial layer cannot fix an access barrier. Review languages, canonicals and sitemaps before multiplying pages.

## When to add GEO tracking

Once you can define relevant buying questions and save comparable responses, observing which brands and sources appear becomes useful. A store with incomplete product data needs to complete its information first; a store with solid information can investigate unanswered questions.

## How to allocate a budget

Commission a diagnosis connecting each query, URL, issue and deliverable. Separate theme fixes, content review and measurement. Avoid paying twice for the same fix because it appears under two acronyms.

[Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) relates AI features to SEO foundations. See the [measurement method](/en/questions/measure-shopify-geo/) and request a [Shopify GEO and SEO audit](/en/services/geo-shopify/).
