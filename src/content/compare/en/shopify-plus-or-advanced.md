## The row that decides it, and it is not the one people look at

Of the whole table, the one that settles 80% of cases is the first: **three active catalogs** on Advanced, unlimited on Plus. And the way to use it is not technical, it is arithmetic.

Take last year's orders and count how many different price lists were **actually** used. Not the ones in the sales PDF. I have seen lists of eight tiers that, once the orders were checked, were two and one exception. If the count is three or fewer, Advanced is enough and the conversation ends.

## The second row is the one that surprises people

Assignment. Without Plus, a [catalog](/en/glossary/b2b-catalog/) is not plugged into a company: it is plugged into a B2B market and the companies in that market inherit it. The consequence is that **your price segmentation has to match your geographic segmentation**.

The example that comes up in almost every meeting: if your three tiers are "wholesale Spain", "wholesale Portugal" and "distributor", they are spent and there is no room for key accounts. Half an hour at a whiteboard before choosing a plan saves the redesign afterwards.

## What is not a reason to move to Plus

- **"We want to customise the checkout."** If what you want is colours, a logo, a font or a notice, that is the editor and public apps, on Advanced.
- **"We are going to grow a lot."** Plus can be bought the day it is needed. The wall appears in the number of price lists long before the number of orders.
- **"We need volume discounts."** They are on Advanced, inside the catalog.

## What is

Your own [Functions](/en/glossary/shopify-functions/). Only stores on Plus can install custom apps that contain them. And that is the difference that breaks the most quotes mid-project: with a public app you adapt to its data model, with your own Function you set the model.

If your cart rule looks at the whole order (a different minimum per company, not mixing tiers, a product that requires another), there is no way to do it without Plus. The exception is one order value minimum for every B2B buyer: since April 2026, Checkout Blocks enforces it on Advanced too.
