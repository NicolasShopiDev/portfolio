Creating a Shopify draft order does not mean its products are reserved. Before saying “I will hold those units”, check the reservation and expiry: preparing an offer and setting goods aside are different decisions.

Sales sent the quote. The customer is thinking. Somebody else just bought the last units.

You do not need a more persuasive email... you need to know what was actually held.

## Separate three promises

A sales conversation may promise a price, an acceptance period and availability. Keep them distinct.

“This offer is valid until Friday” does not explain whether stock is held until then. “We have ten” does not mean ten are reserved for that company.

Write down the offer before configuring anything. Software cannot settle differing interpretations between salespeople on its own.

## What a draft-order reservation does

Shopify’s [draft order guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/create-orders/create-draft) distinguishes unreserved goods, which remain available to other buyers, from reserved items. Reservation requires inventory tracking and allows an expiry date and time through **Reserve inventory**.

That is the check you need. A draft existing or a link being emailed cannot substitute for it.

Open the case and record the reservation shown, references and validity. This article explains the decision rather than claiming the feature has been activated or tested on your store.

## Decide how long to hold merchandise

Hypothetical example: twelve cases remain and a buyer requests eight for a campaign. Your offer allows time to respond, but other companies need the product too.

The question is not merely whether you can click Reserve. It is how long eight cases should be unavailable to other opportunities and who can approve an extension.

You could agree on this record:

| Proposal detail | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| References and quantities | Which goods are being held? |
| Price validity | How long is the offer maintained? |
| Reservation validity | How long are units set aside? |
| Owner | Who can extend or end the commitment? |
| Communication | What has the buyer been told? |

This is a proposed decision template, not a universal commercial policy.

Reserving everything for everyone indefinitely is unnecessary. Sales, warehouse and buyer need the same understanding of the promise.

## Test the journey before a campaign

Prepare a test reference and controlled draft. Record availability before reservation, during it and after expiry. Inspect another buyer’s journey during that period too.

This is a proposed protocol, not an executed test. Agree how to avoid interfering with real orders and retain observed values. Merchandise already promised to a customer is unsuitable for improvisation.

If outcomes differ from expectations, retain the case before correcting it. Reference, timing and saved action are more helpful than “reservations sometimes fail”.

Use Shopify’s [inventory states explanation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inventory-and-locations/fundamentals/inventory-states) to interpret quantities. Different columns need not represent the same measure.

## With an ERP, another half of the test remains

A reservation visible in Shopify does not establish how an ERP, warehouse or external channel receives it. Document the availability owner and expected message for each system.

Ask the connector: does it transmit the commitment, identify its originating draft, handle its end and expose failed delivery? These are requirements to verify, rather than features attributable to every integration.

The [Shopify and ERP diagnostic guide](/en/blog/shopify-erp-sync-errors/) helps trace the data. An apparently sold-out product is a separate symptom covered in the [unbuyable stock guide](/en/blog/shopify-sold-out-with-stock/).

## Reservation, payment and preparation are different

A reservation describes committed merchandise. It does not establish payment, acceptance or an instruction to dispatch immediately.

Define the event authorising the next step. If staff prepare orders from emails without reviewing status, reservation duration cannot fix that process.

Do not mark an order paid merely to make it fit the operation. Status should describe events, rather than whatever makes printing a document convenient.

## What you can organise today

Choose three open quotes. Check promises about price and units, the person following up and the next review time. If answers are missing, start there before automating.

For a [Shopify B2B portal](/en/shopify-b2b-developer/), a useful requirement is “these units are held under these conditions and released through this journey”. Better than “we want everything automatic”.
