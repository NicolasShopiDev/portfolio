If switching language leaves part of your Shopify store in its original language, locate that content before translating everything again. Check its translation and the version buyers see; a language selector cannot establish complete coverage.

English title. English button. Important explanation... still in Spanish.

Customers should not need two languages to decide whether a product suits them.

## Choose one failing sentence and trace it

Keep the page address, selected language and exact sentence. For dropdown content, record how to open it too. “Translation is broken” does little to locate one field among hundreds.

Shopify’s [language guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/languages) explains that missing translations fall back to default-language content. Check that possibility without treating it as the automatic explanation for every block.

Hypothetical example: the main description is translated but an instructions accordion is not. Different sources may supply those sections. Establish what feeds the block rather than assuming the adjacent description shares its translation.

## Identify the resource being translated

Find the sentence in Translate & Adapt and record whether it belongs to product content, theme content or another resource. If an app is involved, establish who manages the wording before changing it elsewhere.

The [manual translation guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/languages/translate-adapt-app/manual-translation) supports working by language and resource. Use that precision for a verifiable correction rather than editing several similar phrases without knowing which reaches the screen.

Do not replace the original text with English merely to fix the screenshot. That can move the same problem to Spanish-speaking buyers. Keep source content and translation distinct.

## Translated, untranslated and outdated are different

Shopify’s [language management guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/languages/manage-languages) distinguishes existing, missing and outdated translations relative to source content. Check that status alongside the affected fragment.

If marketing just changed a product feature, establish what every language should now say. A natural-sounding sentence can still describe an older version.

Prioritise purchasing information: inclusions, sizing, compatibility, usage restrictions and option meanings. A mediocre slogan and an incorrect specification do not have the same impact.

## Automatic does not mean instant or universal

The current [automatic translation guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/languages/translate-adapt-app/automatic-translation) makes features conditional on store availability. Where Auto-sync exists, updates are weekly and initially disabled; enabling it does not translate immediately. Automatically translating does not overwrite manually written or edited translations.

Check the available function and last run before promising that changes will be ready in another language. A campaign starting this afternoon needs a review matching that deadline, rather than reliance on a later update.

Do not reflexively trigger another full-store translation. Establish whether content is missing, outdated or drawn from a different source first.

## Review a buying journey in the chosen language

I propose this matrix for a representative page. These are not measured results or executed store tests:

| Journey stage | Question to check |
| --- | --- |
| Product-page entry | Does the page match the selected language? |
| Product explanation | Does it match current source meaning? |
| Options and dropdowns | Is the selection understandable? |
| Add to cart | Are wording and selected product consistent? |
| Subsequent navigation | Is the expected context preserved? |
| Mobile | Do text and controls remain usable? |

Add current wording, expected wording and a screenshot of the failing section. A correction can then be reviewed without restarting diagnosis.

For differences between preview and publication, use the [theme comparison guide](/en/blog/shopify-theme-preview-live-difference/) to establish the reviewed version. Mixing a test translation with another published page makes a simple issue confusing.

## A button cannot perform the final review

Read as a buyer: is it clear what arrives and which option to choose? When materials, measurements or references require sector knowledge, involve someone who has it.

Word-for-word translation is unnecessary. Preserve meaning and support decisions. A sentence looking like English does not establish that it explains your product well.

For [international Shopify work](/en/services/shopify-markets/), separate content, language and market conditions. Correcting a paragraph does not certify currency, shipping or availability; those need their own checks.

Start today with one product and a second language. Review the matrix and correct one fragment end to end. More useful than a half-translated store with a flawless selector.
