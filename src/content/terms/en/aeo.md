## How to write a useful answer

Answer in the opening sentences, name the product or category and state the condition limiting the answer. Follow with evidence, exceptions and the next step. A compatibility question needs models and requirements rather than a generic statement about quality.

For example, “is it suitable outdoors?” needs approved use and its conditions where the manufacturer supplies those facts. Missing facts must be checked before publication. The answer must remain consistent across languages.

## FAQs and markup do not replace information

FAQs help when they answer distinct questions left open by the main content. Repeating keywords or hiding answers exclusively in JSON-LD does not improve the explanation for buyers.
