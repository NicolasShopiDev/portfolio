## What changes in a Shopify store

A product page should answer buying questions with verifiable facts: use, dimensions, materials, compatibility and limitations. Guides explain how to choose and link to relevant products. Visible content and structured data should describe the same product without duplicate entities or invented reviews.

Technical access is checked before producing more content. Allowing a crawler in robots.txt does not prove that it accessed, indexed or cited a page. For B2B, improving public access must not expose negotiated prices or restricted catalogues.

## GEO, SEO and AEO

SEO addresses search visibility. [AEO](/en/glossary/aeo/) organises direct answers. GEO also examines how a source is mentioned and cited in generated responses. The [SEO and GEO comparison](/en/compare/seo-vs-geo-shopify/) separates goals and metrics.

## What an implementation cannot prove

Publishing FAQs, JSON-LD or llms.txt demonstrates that these components exist. Visibility claims require recorded queries, context, responses and cited URLs. Business claims require visits and enquiries.

[Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) explains how AI features relate to SEO foundations. See the scope of [Shopify GEO consulting](/en/services/geo-shopify/).
